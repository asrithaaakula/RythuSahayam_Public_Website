About RythuSahayam

RythuSahayam is a proposed farmer-support portal for Prathuru and Gundimeda near Tadepalli, Andhra Pradesh. It brings agricultural guidance, scheme information, market links, and a simple problem-reporting facility together in one place.

Prototype note: This is a front-end demonstration. The form downloads a report locally; it does not submit information to an authority. Confirm scheme details and market prices with official sources.
